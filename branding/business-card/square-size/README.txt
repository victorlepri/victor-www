SQUARE BUSINESS CARD
==============================

Canvas at 300 dpi
  bleed  2.71 by 2.71 inches
  trim   2.56 by 2.56 inches
  safe   2.40 by 2.40 inches

The supplied InDesign template is included as template.idml for placement and final shop checks.
The SVG files are editable vector masters. The PNG files are raster proofs. The two page PDF is front first and back second, with the bleed included. print-high-quality.pdf is the vector version for final printing. print.pdf is the raster proof.

The background and pink rule run through the bleed. All name, mark, slogan, contact, and site elements sit 16 pixels inside the safe area for print breathing room.
